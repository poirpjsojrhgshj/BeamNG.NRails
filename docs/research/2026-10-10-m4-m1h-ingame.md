## M4 (512² physics block), tools/m4_probes.py
- P2 GATE PASS: steady 512² importMaps 16.0 ms with the layer map (17.2 without), first 21.6 ms,
  reloadCollision 0.6 ms; the 1024² baseline is 45-48 ms. Extents, overlap heights, restore all
  correct; render-off survives the import.
- P11: physics does NOT follow a re-import without reloadCollision -> keep it (0.6 ms at 512²).
  **Probe bug**: the P11 shrink step made the area outside the 512² block non-collidable while the
  car was 258 m from the centre (the check allowed 300 m), and the user's parked car fell through
  the world (caught and teleported back unharmed). Cause: without reloadCollision physics keeps
  the 1024² block's origin and size over the 512² data, so the ground under any car east / north of
  the centre is gone; a distance limit cannot fix that. Fixed: p11b deactivates the player's car and
  the restore (after reloadCollision) brings it back (mod/tests/test_m4_probes.lua checks both).
- P7b FAIL: a hidden second TerrainBlock collides as soon as it is imported (before any
  reloadCollision), so a background pre-import cannot be silent; the swap itself costs 1.0 ms.
  The double-buffered path is off the table; commits stay import + reloadCollision.
